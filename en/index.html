FAQ

Connection and launch

How do I connect and what do I need?
Connection takes a few steps. You leave a request, a manager contacts you within a day and checks how many branches and platform listings you have. Then we connect Yandex Maps, 2GIS, Google and other platforms, set up roles for your staff and reply rules for the Ody AI agent. After that reviews from all maps appear in one feed, and the team takes a short training on the workspace. The whole process usually takes one to three working days, and you will see the first reply drafts on the day of connection.
